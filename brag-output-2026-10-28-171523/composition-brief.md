## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet field-ledger product film; the object’s story extends past checkout.
- Interpretation: spacious, restrained, readable. Soft crossfades and purposeful row/card movement.
- Angle: a receipt stops at checkout; Kept follows the thing into use and upkeep.
- Hook: “Paid.” followed by “And then?”
- Outro: “Kept. Keep what matters.”
- Avoid: generic SaaS language, abstract filler, unrelated redesign, real user records or private data.
