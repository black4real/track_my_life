# Brag Plan: Kept

## Step 1 — project rubric

1. **What is it?** Kept is a personal ledger for purchases, use, upkeep, routines, and the dates that connect them.
2. **Most impressive claim:** “Remember what you bought, how it’s holding up, and when it needs care.” It ties money, time, and care to the same item record.
3. **Visual hook:** Deep evergreen and warm paper, a lime index rail, large clear item photos, and the dated timeline.
4. **Best real UI:** Library row into a product’s purchase history, then a calendar day and its service entry.
5. **Shortest satisfying video:** 20 seconds; enough time to read one hook, three product moments, and the name.
6. **Tone:** `polished` — a quiet, personal product film; generous holds and restrained movement.
7. **Audio:** Warm, steady instrumental bed (bundled Sascha Ende track, CC BY 4.0); only a few soft interface and card-placement accents.
8. **Caption:** Kept remembers what you paid, how long it stayed, and when it needed care.
9. **User flow:** Find or add an item → record what was paid and its use/service history → revisit the story on its timeline or calendar.

All item names, dates, and amounts shown in the video are fictional demo records created for the composition. No account records, email addresses, secrets, or personal photos are used.

## The angle

A receipt stops at checkout. Kept follows the thing home: one calm record connects what it cost, how it held up, and when it needed care. The film moves from a single purchase into a readable history, then opens that history into the calendar and routines.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)

On warm paper, a small receipt line lands: “Paid.” A second line appears underneath: “And then?” Cut to Kept’s own quiet ledger UI.

## Key moments (the middle)

- Library: a fictional charcoal hoodie has its own photo, category, and ownership age.
- Product history: two fictional purchase entries keep their totals and quantities separate, so a repeat purchase does not overwrite the last one.
- Calendar and Routine: dated records and morning/night product steps appear as connected parts of a personal history.

## Outro / punchline

Kept. “Keep what matters.” Hold the wordmark and let the music resolve.

## User flow worth showing

Find an item → add dated paid details → revisit its history in the calendar. The purchase totals are shown as demo data, not a real user’s account.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: a quiet field-ledger product film; the object’s story extends past checkout.
- Interpretation: slow enough to read, with soft crossfades and small physical-feeling placements instead of a flashy tech montage.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Share copy (draft)

Kept remembers what you paid, how long it stayed, and when it needed care.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, restrained instrumental bed
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`
- Music treatment: start under the receipt hook, keep it low beneath the product story, fade through the final wordmark.
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset, estimated 110 BPM; strong cues at 8.74s, 13.11s, and 17.47s. Treat as optional; readability comes first.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none; keep this film still and confident.
- SFX posture: sparse, soft card placements and one light UI click.
- Audio-coupled moments: gentle placement for the two purchase entries; soft click when the calendar day opens.
- Restraint rule: no loud dings, aggressive swishes, or sound that competes with the copy.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — After checkout — 3.5s
A narrow receipt line sits in quiet paper space: “Paid.” Then “And then?” appears beneath it. Brief settle; cut to the real Kept palette and logo mark.
Sequential/interaction: yes — one line, then the next, held together long enough to read.
Audio intent: warm bed begins quietly; a single soft paper/card placement.
Audio-coupled idea: accent the second line, not every letter.
Music: warm, steady instrumental.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — The library — 4s
Recreate Kept’s actual desktop library shape: indexed rows, search, leaf accent, and a selected item detail. The fictional “Charcoal hoodie” row settles into focus with “Clothes · Hoodie” and “2 months”.
Sequential/interaction: yes — row appears, then selection rail moves into place.
Audio intent: music stays warm; one subtle UI selection sound.
Audio-coupled idea: selected row lands on a nearby beat if it does not shorten the read.
Music: same bed.
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — The long story — 5s
Show the real purchase-history card and timeline pattern with fictional records: “2 items · ₹1,200 total” and “1 item · ₹680 total.” Dates are clearly demo dates. Emphasize that each paid total stays attached to its own purchase.
Sequential/interaction: yes — two history entries arrive one by one, then hold together.
Audio intent: a soft card placement for the second entry.
Audio-coupled idea: entries arrive near separate beats, then hold for reading.
Music: same bed.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — Dates, kept — 4s
Recreate the actual month calendar with photos/dots and a selected day panel; one fictional service record appears. A small Routine view slides in beside it: Morning / Cleanser / Night. This supports that Kept keeps upkeep and routines in the same dated world.
Sequential/interaction: yes — calendar date opens, then one routine row appears.
Audio intent: one quiet click on the selected date; no extra flourish.
Audio-coupled idea: calendar day opens on a strong cue, then text holds.
Music: same bed.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 5.

### Scene 5 — Keep what matters — 3.5s
Return to the night-paper surface. The Kept wordmark and real tagline “Keep what matters.” hold cleanly at center.
Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: music fades gently under the wordmark.
Audio-coupled idea: final logo settles on the closing phrase.
Music: fade to silence at the end.
Transition mood: fade out.

**Music mood for this video:** polished, warm, quietly optimistic.
**Audio summary:** a steady instrumental bed carries a few soft, motion-matched taps and fades under the final wordmark.
